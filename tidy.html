Tidy up

Looks for data left behind by earlier versions — wrong round counts, handicaps calculated from rounds that no longer exist, duplicate golfers, groups nobody can reach. It shows you everything it finds before changing anything.

Nothing is deleted without showing you first. Rounds, golfers and courses are never removed — only counts and handicaps are corrected, and only records that point at things which no longer exist are cleared.
