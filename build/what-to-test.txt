The Scorecard 2.31.5 — sign-in emails sent by The Scorecard itself

1. Forgot password: on the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password and send it to your own address. The email comes from The Scorecard (thescorecard.marker@gmail.com), titled Choose a new password for The Scorecard. Tap its button: The Scorecard opens with no Firebase page in between, and lets you choose a new password.
2. Approval: approve a test applicant. Their email is titled Welcome to The Scorecard and opens the app the same way.
3. Cockpit: your own row shows your golfer name, never your email. The small cnt.txt note under the list is gone.
4. The camera and scorecard photo reading from 2.31.4 still work (Manage > Add a course > Take a photo).
