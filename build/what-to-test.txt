The Scorecard Version 2.0 (2.30.0-beta.7): tools inside the app, and smoother screens.

1. Admin, Cockpit, Open Tidy, and Admin, Settings, Rebuild the roster and Clean up unused groups: each opens inside the app, already signed in, with "Back to the app" at the top. None opens Safari any more.
2. On those tool pages you can scroll straight away.
3. After "Back to the app", the app should open quickly, not stay on "Checking your access". If it is ever slow, tap Send a report: it now lists how long each start-up step took.
4. First screen (signed out): "Become a member or start a group" is now above "I have a code".
5. Sign-in card: shorter. The old "Set your email and password" screen (old guest sign-ins only) is shorter too.
6. Still in this version: Terms of Use on first open, the short invitation screen, fixed-size bottom tabs, pinch zoom, and no version 1 import.
