The Scorecard Version 2.0 (2.30.0) — build 7, with the go-live fixes.

Before go-live (the Version 2.0 rules are not published yet) only the owner should test, and only these:
1. Signed out, the app shows Sign in, "Apply to join the public group" and "I was given a code". Nobody is signed in by itself.
2. Sign in with your email and Scorecard password. Your groups, roster, History and handicaps appear.
3. Post a round and see it in History; your handicap updates.
(Group creation, admin invitations and the public group need the Version 2.0 rules, so they wait for go-live.)

After go-live (go2.txt has run), also check:
4. Tap the group name: only the owner sees "Start another group". The Invite sheet offers Admin only to the owner.
5. Apply to the public group from a second device with a test email. The application appears on the owner's Admin tab. Approve it; the email arrives; choose a password; sign in; the test account is in the public group. Until they join, they are listed under "Approved, not joined yet" with "Send the email again".
5b. Send a named invitation to a test golfer and open the link signed out on another device: it greets them by name and group after they create their account.
6. As that test account: the group ranking shows names and indexes; "Report or block" works; a blocked name disappears from your screens.
7. As the owner: the report appears on the Admin tab; Dismiss it.
8. A regular member sees only their own rounds and the games they played in.
9. An old guest sign-in sees "Set your email and password" and keeps their place after setting them.
10. Delete my account: only with a test account, never your own.
