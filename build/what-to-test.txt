The Scorecard Version 2.0 (2.30.0-beta.3): the new first screen and the private group requests.

1. Signed out, the first screen shows only Sign in (email, password, Sign in, Forgot the password) and two buttons: "I have a code" and "Become a member or start a group".
2. "I have a code": type the code, Continue, then create your account or sign in; the code is filled in for you.
3. "Become a member or start a group" offers two choices. "Become a member" is the public group application; "Start your own group" is the private group request. Each shows What happens next and the Conditions, and is sent only when the box is ticked.
4. Send a group request from a second device with a test email. As the owner, it appears on the Admin tab under Group requests. Approve it: the group is created and an email to the organiser opens with the admin invitation link. Decline sends no email.
5. Open that link on the second device: create the account and join the new group as its admin.
6. Everything from build 7 still works: sign in, post a round, the public group, invitations.
