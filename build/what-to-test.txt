The Scorecard Version 2.0 (2.30.0-beta.8): no more waiting after Tidy, and one less invitation screen.

1. Open Tidy (Admin, Cockpit), then tap "Back to the app". The app should open within a few seconds, with no stop on "Loading your group" and no need to switch apps. The same for Rebuild the roster and Clean up unused groups.
2. Invitation with an email that already has an account: type that email and its password twice on the invitation screen and tap Create account. You go straight to the invitation, with no second sign-in screen. A wrong password is explained on the same screen.
3. If anything is ever slow, tap Send a report: it lists how long each start-up step took, and how long closing took when you left for a tool.
4. Still in this version: tools inside the app, Terms of Use on first open, fixed-size bottom tabs, pinch zoom.

Report problems with Send a report in the app.
