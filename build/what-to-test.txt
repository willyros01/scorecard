The Scorecard Version 2.0 (2.30.0-beta.4): the cockpit and the applications switch.

1. Admin now has tabs at the top: Cockpit, Applications, Members, Settings. Members is People, exactly as before. Settings holds Group, Backup, Course lookup and Account.
2. Cockpit (owner): members and sign-ins across all groups, what is waiting for you, every group (tap one to see it), rounds per month, housekeeping. Last seen starts counting from this version.
3. A group admin sees the Cockpit of their own group and the Members tab.
4. Summary: a "My season" card with your rounds this year and your index.
5. Applications (public group open): the Manual / Auto switch, applications with the name already numbered when taken, reports, and the block list.
6. Become a member with an email that already has an account: the form says so at once and nothing is sent.
7. With the switch on Auto, apply from a second device with a test email: a link arrives; tap it, choose a password, and the test account is in the public group. Turn the switch back to Manual afterwards.
8. The header stays the same size whatever the A / A+ / A++ setting; a long press on a tab name no longer selects it.
