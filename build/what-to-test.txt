The Scorecard 2.31.1, 6 November: the fixes from testing 2.31.0 (the rules are the same as 2.31.0; no script to run).

1. Invitation on a phone where you are signed in: if your account already plays as another golfer, the app signs you out by itself and opens the new person's welcome screen. Otherwise it asks; "No, it's for someone else" signs out and continues at once.
2. As the group owner: invitation link, then sign out for the new person: no box covers the Join button; Join works.
3. With the invitation message open, tap an invitation link: the box closes and the link's screen shows.
4. Forms (apply, request a group, welcome screen, sign in): a mistake (wrong email, second password empty, box not ticked) shows a pop-up in the middle that stays until OK; then the box to fix is outlined.
5. My rounds: the scoring chart has a colour for each kind of score, by day and by night.
6. Enter: "Find another course" is the first line of the Course list; there is no separate link.
7. A Join that fails (for example a replaced invitation) leaves no account behind and says why.
8. A refused join says why in plain words, never "permission-denied".
