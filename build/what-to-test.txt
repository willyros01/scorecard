The Scorecard for iPhone and iPad — first TestFlight build.

Please check, on your own device:
1. Sign in with your usual email and Scorecard password. Your group, roster, History and handicap appear exactly as in the web app.
2. Tap an invitation link sent from the app: it should open the app on the join screen.
3. Post a round with Airplane Mode on, close the app, reopen it, turn the network back on: the round uploads once.
4. Backup: Save it somewhere, then Save to Files. The file appears in iCloud Drive.
5. Share a summary by WhatsApp, Email, Text message and Copy.
6. There is no Google sign-in button anywhere.

Keep using the web app as well until everything above works. Report problems with Send a report in the app.
