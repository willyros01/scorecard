The Scorecard 2.30.0, build of 22 October: the entry screens.

Use a test group or test emails only, never Golfing Buddies' real rounds.

1. Approve a public application (Admin, Applications). The applicant gets an email titled "Sign in to The Scorecard", not "Reset your password".
2. Tap that link: "Create your password" appears once, with the email filled in. Save it: you are in the public group.
3. Tap the same link again: "This link no longer works", with the email filled in, Send me a new link, and Sign in.
4. Signed in as yourself, open an invitation meant for somebody else: "Is this invitation for you?" Tap No: "This link is for a new person". Cancel keeps you signed in. Sign out and continue opens the new person's Create account.
5. Signed in, open an approval link sent to another email: "This link is for a new person" straight away.
6. Turn on Airplane mode and open the app signed in: never "Start your group"; you see "You're offline" or "Can't check your account right now".
7. Signed out, the first screen says "Become a member or request a group", and the choice says "Request your own group".
8. Everything after signing in (Enter, History, Summary, Games, Manage, Admin) is unchanged.

No Firebase rule, index or data change is required. Report problems with Send a report in the app.
