The Scorecard 2.31.0, 5 November: the whole update (batches 1 to 6). Before testing, run r31.txt in Cloud Shell (the 2.31.0 rules).

1. Look: Lake blue, the new icon, day and night (tap Day / Night at the top).
2. Forgot password (Sign in screen): the email "Sign in to The Scorecard" arrives; its link opens the app at "Choose a new password". (Until release day the link may open a web page first: the email-link switch is the release-day step.)
3. Invite a golfer (Manage, Show, Invite): their message names the App Store and the Android download; their link opens "Welcome to <group>", email, password twice, Join, and they are in.
4. Apply to the public group with a name that is already a golfer's: the warning comes at once.
5. Edit and delete a round: the golfer's index and the group list change at once.
6. A round from The Marker: its scores are read-only here; notes and game can change. Tap Card under it: the hole-by-hole card.
7. My rounds: the handicap trend and the scoring statistics under it.
8. Games: delete a game (Edit, Delete this game): its rounds stay, out of the game.
9. Enter: only your own courses, and Find another course for any other (search works for every golfer).
10. Admin (app owner): open a group you are not in; Settings, Course search key: paste a fresh key.

Report problems with Send a report in the app.
