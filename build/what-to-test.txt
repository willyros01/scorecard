The Scorecard 2.30.0-beta.14: favourite courses.

1. On Enter, choose a course, then tap "Add to my favourites" under it. The button changes to "In my favourites - tap to remove".
2. Open the course list again: it starts with "My favourites", then "Played most" (the courses with the most rounds you can see), then "All courses" A to Z.
3. Sign in with the same account on the website (or another device): the same favourites head the list.
4. Remove a favourite in the app: it disappears from the website's list too.
5. The same grouped list appears in Games (new game and edit game) and in the History filter.
6. Without a signal, a favourite change shows at once and is saved when the signal returns.

No Firebase rule, index or data change is required. Report problems with Send a report in the app.
