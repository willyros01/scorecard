The Scorecard

Getting started

Everything from opening it the first time to running a tournament. No knowledge assumed — if you can send a text message, you can use this.

1What this is

A golf handicap tracker your group shares. Somebody posts the scores after a round, and everyone's handicap keeps itself up to date.

It is a website that behaves like an app. There is nothing to buy and nothing to install from an app store. You open a web address once, add it to your home screen, and after that it opens like any other app — including when you have no signal.

3Putting it on your device

You only do this once. Afterwards it sits with your other apps and opens with one tap.

Why bother? Because it opens full screen without the browser's bars around it, it starts faster, and it keeps working when you have no signal out on the course.

iPhone and iPad

1

Open it in Safari

It must be Safari. Chrome on an iPhone cannot add apps to the home screen.

2

Tap the Share button

A square with an arrow pointing up out of it. On an iPhone it is at the bottom of the screen; on an iPad it is at the top right.

3

Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen

The list is long. Keep scrolling — it is well down, past Add Bookmark and Add to Favourites.

4

Tap Add

Top right corner. The icon appears on your home screen with your other apps.

The one thing to know about iPhones and iPads. Safari and the home-screen icon are treated as two separate places. If you use both, sign in on each — otherwise the app thinks you are two different people. Guests who joined by an invitation link need do nothing.

If it does not appear, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

4Joining your group

Almost everybody joins by tapping a link. There is nothing to type and no password.

If you were given a code instead

Type your name exactly as it appears on your group's list, then the six-character code. Spelling matters — "Bob" and "Robert Tan" become two different people with two different handicaps.

Using more than one device

Tap the same invitation link on each of them. Your link keeps working for you.

5Posting a round

Two ways to enter a round

At the foot of the Enter tab there is a link that switches between them, and the app remembers which you chose.

  • Every field at once — the original. Best for typing in a whole fourball's cards after a game.
  • One step at a time — golfer, then course and tees, then the score, each on its own with much larger type. Best for posting your own round on a phone.

The fields, the order and the checks are identical. Only the layout differs, so a round posted either way is exactly the same round.

Adjusted score

Leave it blank almost always. It exists for the official rule that caps each hole at net double bogey. If you do not already know you need it, you do not need it.

If you have no signal

Post anyway. The round is kept on your phone and uploads by itself when signal returns. The badge at the top right turns red while anything is waiting.

6Your handicap

Open Summary. After three rounds your Handicap Index appears. Before that it tells you how many more you need.

TermWhat it means
Handicap IndexYour ability as one number, the same at every course. This is the one people mean by "my handicap".
Course HandicapHow many strokes you get at this course from these tees. Worked out from your index.
GrossYour actual score.
NetYour score minus your course handicap. This is what makes a game between different abilities fair.
DifferentialHow well you played that day, adjusted for how hard the course was. Your index comes from your best eight of the last twenty.

The trend chart

Open My rounds and it appears at the top.

7For whoever runs the group

Everything above, plus three tabs guests never see: Manage, Admin, and the full History.

Before the first outing

1

Add every golfer

Manage, type the name, Add golfer. Include people without phones — they can be scored without ever opening the app.

Setting up a second group? Add from my other groups brings people across with their rounds and handicap. Never retype a name — that creates a second person with half their history.

2

Add your courses

Manage, Add a course. Search by name, or type the rating and slope from the scorecard.

3

Invite everybody

Manage, then Invite beside each name. The link names them, so they tap once and are in. Choose guest or admin, then WhatsApp, email or text.

Somebody who already has a handicap

Manage, Index beside their name, and type it in. It stands in until they have three rounds with you, then their own rounds take over completely.

Guest, admin, owner

RoleCan doPassword?
GuestPosts their own rounds; sees results, rankings and their own history.None
AdminAlso adds courses and golfers, creates games, posts for anybody, edits any round.Yes
OwnerEverything, plus invitations, roles, backup and settings. Cannot be demoted.Yes
Admins are asked for a password as they join. The form appears straight after tapping the invitation — nothing to go and find. Not the password for their email — a new one, for this app only. They can choose Not now, but until they set it their role works in that one browser only: it cannot follow them to another device, and it is lost if the browser's data is cleared. Guests are never asked for one.
An admin link is a different link. A guest cannot turn theirs into an admin one. A guest link keeps working for that same person on another device; an admin link works once.

Running a game

  1. Games, Add a game. Set the date and course. For a tournament, tap Runs over more than one day and set the last day.
  2. Everyone posts their round as normal. They do not have to pick the game.
  3. Open the game afterwards. Every round played on those dates is offered — tick and add in one tap.
  4. Share the result. Tick net, gross, or both.
Set somebody's handicap after the game already existed? Open the game and tap Recalculate this game's handicaps. A round remembers the handicap that applied when it was posted — that is what stops old results shifting — so an index set afterwards does not reach back into it. Recalculating shows you every change before making it.

Nothing you can tap in the app deletes a golfer or their rounds. Remove only takes somebody off a roster. Their record, rounds and handicap stay where they are.

Before you hand it to the group

  • The app opens and shows no red bar.
  • Your courses and roster are in, under Manage.
  • You have taken a backup and saved it somewhere you will find it.
  • You have joined on a second device and it shows the same rounds.
  • One friend has joined and can post a round — and cannot see Manage or Admin.
  • Airplane mode: post a round, the badge goes red, turn it off, it uploads.

Then send the invitations.
