For everyone playing

Quick start

Everything you need. There is nothing else to learn.

Getting in

  1. Tap the link you were sent.
  2. Type the name you play under.
  3. Tap Start. That is it — no account, no password.
Add it to your home screen so you can find it again.
iPhone or iPad: tap the share button, scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen.
Android: tap the menu, then Install app.

Posting your round

  1. Open Enter.
  2. Your name is already filled in.
  3. Pick the course, then the tees you played.
  4. Type your score.
  5. Tap Post round.

Made a mistake? Go to My rounds and tap Edit. You have 24 hours; after that ask whoever runs the group.

Your handicap

Open Summary. After three rounds your Handicap Index appears. Before that it tells you how many more you need.

It uses the World Handicap System — the average of your best 8 rounds out of your last 20. If you play in more than one group, all of them count towards the same index.

About the trend chart. The line is your Handicap Index after each round. Filled dots are the rounds currently counting — the best 8 of your last 20. Hollow dots are still in the window but are not affecting your number today. The dashed line marks your lowest.

Net is your score minus your handicap — that is what the ranking uses, so everyone competes fairly. Gross is your raw score, shown beside it.

No signal on the course? Post anyway. Rounds are saved on your phone and upload by themselves when you get signal back. The badge at the top right turns red while they are waiting.

Cannot see a Manage tab? That is normal. Only organisers have it.

For organisers

Quick start

Everything above, plus the parts only you can reach.

Before the first outing

  1. Manage → add every golfer, including those without phones. They can be scored without ever opening the app.
  2. Manage → add your courses. Search by name, or type the rating and slope off the scorecard.
  3. Manage → Invite beside a golfer's name. The link names them, so they tap it once and they are in — nothing to type, and no chance of a misspelled second record with a split handicap. Choose guest or admin, then pick WhatsApp, email, text or anything else. The message includes a link to this guide.
Spell names the same way. When someone joins, the app matches their name against your roster. "Bob" and "Robert Tan" become two people with two handicaps. Agree the spelling and stick to it.

Roles

RoleCan do
GuestPosts their own rounds, sees results and rankings.
AdminAlso adds courses and golfers, creates games, posts for anybody, edits any round.
YouEverything, plus invitations, roles, backup and settings.

To promote somebody who has already joined: Admin → tap Make admin beside their name. Or invite them as an admin in the first place, and they arrive with the role already set.

Guests need no password. Admins do. A guest taps their link and is in. An admin is asked to set a password, because their link, if it went astray, would hand over the roster. A guest link keeps working for that same person on another device; an admin link works once.
An admin invitation is a different link. Nobody can turn a guest link into an admin one — they carry different secrets, and the database checks which was used.

On the day

  1. Games → Add a game. Set the date and course.
  2. Everyone posts their round. They can pick the game, but they do not have to.
  3. Open the game afterwards. Any round played on that date is offered — tick and add in one tap.
  4. Share the result when you are done.
A tournament over several days? When adding the game, tap Runs over more than one day and set the last day. Any round played on any day in that range can be added to it. Open the game and you get a Standing — every player, their score each day, and a running total, with the leader at the top. Tap Edit on a game to change its name, dates or course afterwards.

You can also take a round back out of a game — open the game and tap Remove beside the name. The round itself and the golfer's handicap are untouched.

One person, one handicap. A golfer who plays in two of your groups is one record. Their index is built from all their rounds, wherever they played. Rename them once and it changes everywhere.
Deleting a group. Its rounds and games go with it. Golfers and courses never do — they belong to you, not to a group, and their handicaps are rebuilt from whatever rounds remain. A copy of the rounds is saved to the device first and offered to you as a file, so even this is recoverable. Only the owner can delete a group.
